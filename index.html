Margin note

N.C. Gen. Stat. § 58‑3‑180(a)

“A policy covering damage to a motor vehicle shall allow the claimant to select the repair service or source for the repair of the damage.”

In plain words: your insurer can recommend a shop. It can’t make you use it.

Why it matters

The shop works for the person who owns the car.

Apex Auto Body Specialists has billed itself as an independent shop for years. Its old website put it bluntly: Work for you (the insured or claimant), not the Insurance Company.

It also said the shop works with the major insurance companies, just not under contract to them. That means you bring the estimate, the photos and the claim number, and Darin’s crew fixes the car.
