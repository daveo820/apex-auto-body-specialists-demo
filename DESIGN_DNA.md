# Design DNA: Apex Auto Body Specialists

- **Hero archetype:** manifesto with a statute margin note. A big two line statement on asphalt black, and to the right a "margin note" card quoting N.C. Gen. Stat. 58-3-180(a). New in the portfolio (bb type led job log, absolute edge bleed, cfr engineering report, carroll storefront, core-home editorial split, edgewood scorecard collage, hollingsworth full bleed photo, red3 spec sheet, triple-crown asymmetric panel, wolf magazine cover).
- **Signature motif:** the pinstripe. Two coach lines, blue over orange, that draw in from the left when they scroll into view. Used under the hero, under every subpage header, in kickers, beside the review heading, and on top of the footer (5 uses). Nods to the blue striped header on the shop's old site.
- **Motion:** fade plus 22px rise on `.rv`; pinstripe scaleX draw. No blur. Reduced motion shows everything at once.
- **Type:** Chakra Petch 600/700 (display, a squared, machined face that reads automotive without being a racing cliché) plus Source Sans 3 (body, 18px, line height 1.62). Both self-hosted woff2 latin subsets, preloaded.

- **Section shapes:** hero split 1.55 to 1; "why" two column with a numbered process ledger; services as a dark 3 by 2 board; reviews with a lead card; footer as a closing spread ("Call the shop before you call it settled").
- **Buttons:** square corners, signal orange with asphalt text; outline variant.
- **Placeholders:** black tag with orange "PLACEHOLDER" text plus a hatched box for photos.
